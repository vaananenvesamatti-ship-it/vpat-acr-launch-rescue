Do I actually need a VPAT?

Four questions. There's a fair chance the answer is no — in which case this page will tell you so and stop, rather than selling you something.

1. Who asked you for it?
2. What exactly did they ask for?
3. Has your product's accessibility ever been tested?
4. Would being wrong be expensive?

For example: a contractual conformance commitment, a regulated sector, a very large deal, or prior legal complaints about accessibility.

This is a neutral orientation tool, not legal advice and not an accessibility assessment. It cannot tell you whether your product conforms to anything — only what the request you received probably means and what a sensible next step looks like.
